Range

Range on a mesh network is governed by node density more than by the output power of any single unit. Coverage is extended by adding nodes, not by increasing power at one of them. All figures below are single links between two radios, and are drawn from the field tests documented on the Test Data page.

WiFi mesh Measured range
50 m elevation difference, clear line of sight 5 km
Ground level 1 km
Heavy obstruction, buildings or dense tree cover 100 m
LoRa Measured range
With elevation, longest test conducted 22 miles, link held
With elevation, secondary test 8 miles, link held
Maximum range Not established in testing to date

LoRa results were measured on 159 mW radios. V3 ships a 1,000 mW LoRa radio, not yet range tested.
