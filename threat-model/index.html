Threat model  /  Limits

What Nucleus protects, and what it doesn't

Nucleus encrypts traffic on the mesh and keeps local communication off carriers and cloud services. It doesn't make you invisible, and it can't stop someone who holds a node or a phone.

01Protects

What it protects against

ThreatHow
Reading traffic on the WiFi mesh802.11s with WPA3 encrypts every link between nodes.
Reading Reticulum messagesEnd-to-end encryption with forward secrecy. Nodes in between carry ciphertext. See private network.
Depending on carriers or cloud servicesLocal communication runs on your own nodes, with no internet connection and no accounts.
Losing one pathText goes out over WiFi and LoRa. If one is down, the other can still deliver it.

02Limits

What it doesn't protect against

ThreatWhy
RF detection and direction-findingEvery transmitter can be detected and located, including WiFi and LoRa.
JammingA radio path can be jammed. Traffic can move to another path, but the jammed one is denied.
LoRa readers on your channelAny Meshtastic radio on the same channel can read and answer your messages. Packet headers, such as the sending node, are not encrypted.
Tailscale metadataIf you log in to Tailscale, its coordination server sees which devices are in your network.
A seized nodeAssume whoever holds a node can read its configuration and the messages stored on it.
A compromised phone or laptopEncryption ends on the device. If the device is compromised, so are its messages.
