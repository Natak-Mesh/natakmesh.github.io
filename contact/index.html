02Questions

Common questions

How do I buy a Nucleus?

From our online store. For larger orders, email us.

How many do I need?

At least two. A mesh needs two nodes to form, and every node you add extends coverage.

What support do you offer?

Email us with any problem. Setup, troubleshooting or a question about how something works.

How do updates work?

The node's System Update page checks GitHub for a newer version of V3 OS. Press Update now and the node installs it and restarts its services. It needs an internet connection, for example through the Ethernet port. Your settings are kept.

Is there a manual?

Yes. There's a short manual on this site, and the full operator manual, one page per web UI screen, is on GitHub.
